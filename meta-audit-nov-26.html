Rules

How we call it

New ads
  • Video hook rate 25%+ by 48 hours
  • Click-through 1.2%+ by 72 hours
  • Cost per landing page view $2.50 or less
  • Off at $150 with no purchase
  • Into Core at 3+ purchases under $75
Budgets
  • Up 20% max every 72 hours while under $75
  • Hold between $75 and $100
  • Down 20% if over $100 for a week
  • No edits to winners during BFCM
  • $75 happy, $100 acceptable, $117 ceiling
